# CARIFTA 2027 selection rules update

Source: official ASAJ 2026 Carifta Criteria.pdf, ratified 3 December 2025.

- Only date-verified LCM results from 1 July 2026 are used for the CARIFTA 2027 qualifying assessment. Final local qualifying meet date is pending.
- Earlier results remain in Race History and Jamaica Rankings for historical comparison.
- Ranking snapshot source_date does not prove the date of the swim. Thus a September ranking best is not automatically treated as an eligible performance.
- Time qualification, 1st/2nd-place selection priority and final team selection are distinct. Current ranking, eligibility, all-team candidates and AQUA points are required before selection can be projected.
- Team limits: 8 per age group/gender, 36 overall; relay additions have separate rules.
- Goodwill logic and standards are unchanged.
- The rule document is labelled 2026, although applied here to the 2027 qualifying period; confirm with ASAJ if revised 2027 criteria are issued.
