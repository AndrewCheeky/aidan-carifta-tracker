How to read this tracker

CARIFTA A and B standards are qualifying-time benchmarks, not guaranteed selection. Under the supplied criteria, qualifying-period ranking and other eligibility requirements matter. The 29 Sep 2026 rankings are a historical snapshot. Calculated rankings use imported ASAJ-verified LCM races and recorded ages at each meet; they are not official current-age national rankings or selection rankings. An overall PB may have been achieved outside the eligible period. ASAJ LCM evidence and unverified SwimCloud SCM results are never combined. Meet-range dates do not establish an exact race day. This independent tracker is not an official ASAJ publication.

Progress bars show improvement from the swimmer's earliest documented comparable ASAJ LCM time to the B standard. They are hidden if that baseline or the target is unavailable, or if the baseline was already faster than the B standard. Rankings and times link to their original sources where provided; the supplied September ranking PDF does not include a page reference in every source CSV.

Privacy: This public website identifies a minor and displays published competitive swimming results. The owner should review consent and visibility regularly. · Independent tracker · Not an ASAJ selection decision.
